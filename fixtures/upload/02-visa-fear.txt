Counselor: Last time you mentioned a job offer. How did that go?
Student: They want me to start next week, twenty hours, but I am scared to take it.
Counselor: Tell me about the fear.
Student: I heard if you work off campus you can lose your visa. I do not understand what is allowed and what is not, and everyone tells me something different.
Counselor: That sounds stressful.
Student: I keep thinking one wrong shift and I will be sent home. I can't sleep.
Counselor: Have you asked the international office?
Student: I went once. They said to read the website. The website says one thing and my friend says another.
Counselor: And money?
Student: My parents send what they can. Without this job I will have to skip meals at the end of the month. But I am not allowed to work this many hours, so I have to say no.
