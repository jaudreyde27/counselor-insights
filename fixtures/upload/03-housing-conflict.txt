Counselor: How is the new apartment working out?
Student: Not great. My roommates stay up until three in the morning and I cannot study.
Counselor: Have you talked with them about it?
Student: I tried once. They said I am too sensitive, so now I just stay quiet.
Counselor: How does the quiet feel?
Student: I feel like an outsider in my own home. Nobody asks me to join dinner.
Counselor: What about the lease?
Student: I signed for twelve months and the rent is more than I planned. If I move I lose the deposit.
Counselor: Is money a worry for you?
Student: Every week. My scholarship covers tuition but not rent, and I do not know how to ask the housing office for help.
Counselor: What would asking look like?
Student: I do not even know which office to go to. I'm embarrassed to ask again after they gave me the wrong form.
